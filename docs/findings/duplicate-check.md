## What the ML-fairness duplicate affected

The two AI Fairness 360 records produced **4 of the 95** shortlisted
claim pairs in ML fairness, all classified `supports`: the paper agreeing
with its own preprint. They are not contradictions and feed no gap card
(ML fairness has none), but any count of supporting pairs for that
library is inflated by 4. They were left in place; the corpus is not
changed in this run.

## Should the 4-pass dedup have collapsed it? Why it did not

Two reasons, both checked in code:

1. **The 4-pass dedup was never run on the two new libraries.**
   `normalizer.deduplicate()` runs in `build_expanded_manifest.py`
   (LLM calibration only). The multi-domain pipeline
   (`multi_domain.py`) keeps papers unique by OpenAlex work ID only
   (`seen_ids`), and its prelabel output's `n_pre_dedup` field is the
   count before trimming, not before any dedup.
2. **Even replayed, the 4-pass dedup does not merge this pair** (the
   table above shows no collapses in any library). Pass 1 needs the same
   DOI (they differ: `10.48550/arxiv.1810.01943` vs
   `10.1147/jrd.2019.2942287`). Passes 2–4 need the same normalized
   title, and the journal version dropped "Understanding" and "Unwanted".
   Pass 4 is the preprint/published pass, but it too requires identical
   titles.

So by its own rules the dedup is behaving as designed; by its purpose
(no work counted as two independent papers) it should catch this case.
A fix would need a title-similarity rule for arXiv/non-arXiv DOI pairs
with shared authors. Changing the dedup policy is a decision for
`docs/merge-policy.md` and is not made here.
